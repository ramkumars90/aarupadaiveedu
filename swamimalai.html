Swamimalai சுவாமிமலை

Also known as Thiruveragam (திருவேரகம்), its name in Thirumurugatrupadai. Also spelt Suvamimalai. Murugan here is worshipped as Swaminatha Swamy.

On the banks of the Kaveri near Kumbakonam, the child Murugan sits as Swaminatha, the guru who taught his own father the meaning of Om.

The story of this abode

When Brahma, the creator, could not explain the meaning of the sacred syllable Om to the child Murugan, the boy imprisoned him and took over creation himself. Shiva came to ask for Brahma's release and asked his son what Om meant.

Murugan said he would explain only if Shiva listened as a student. Shiva sat before him, and Murugan whispered the meaning in his ear. From that day he is Swaminatha, the guru of Shiva, or simply Thagappan Swami, the father's teacher.

There are no natural hills in this flat delta, so the temple stands on a built-up hillock about 60 feet high, a kattu malai.

What to look for

  • The 60 steps, each named after one year of the 60-year Tamil calendar cycle.
  • Meenakshi and Sundareswarar (Shiva) in the shrine at the foot, below their son.
  • An elephant, not a peacock, shown as Murugan's mount here.
  • Swamimalai's bronze-casting workshops in the village, famous for temple icons.

Rituals explained

Prayers for learning

As the abode of Murugan the teacher, many families pray here for their children's studies.

Archanai at the counter.

Climbing the 60 steps

Many climb slowly, touching each step, which stands for one Tamil year.

Free.

Mottai (head-shaving)

Hair offering at the tonsure hall.

Free at HR&CE temples. Take the official receipt.

Archanai

Chanting of the Lord's names with your name and birth star.

Small fee at the counter.

Facilities and accessibility

Moderate: 60 steps, or the lift

Sixty broad steps lead to the main shrine, in two flights of 30 with a landing between. Most elderly visitors manage with rests, and the temple also has a lift for those who can't manage the steps. Ask at the base.

Steps60, in two flights of 30.
LiftA lift to the hilltop shrine, for elderly visitors and anyone who can't climb.
Shiva shrineAt ground level, no steps.

Where to stay and eat

Stay

Stay in Kumbakonam, the temple town 8–9 km away, which has hotels and lodges at every budget.

Food

Kumbakonam is known for its filter coffee and vegetarian meals. A few small eateries are near the temple.

Before you go

  • Visit between October and March, when the steps are cool.
  • Base yourself in Kumbakonam and combine with the Navagraha temples.
  • Pray here for studies and wisdom; this is Murugan the teacher.
  • Look for the bronze workshops on the way into the village.

Our tried-and-tested picks

From our own visit, October 2025

Stay

KVM Hotels, Srirangam, Tiruchirappalli. Right in front of the river Kaveri, though the rooms have no river view.

Getting there

We took a cab from Srirangam to the temple. If you'd rather not travel that far, Kumbakonam is the closer base.

Eat

Simple roadside eateries near the Srirangam temple.

How to reach

Directions in Google Maps

By train

Kumbakonam station is about 9 km away, with trains from Chennai, Thanjavur and Tiruchirappalli.

By air

Tiruchirappalli airport is about 90 km away.

By road

Town buses, autos and taxis run from Kumbakonam. Thanjavur is about 35 km.
