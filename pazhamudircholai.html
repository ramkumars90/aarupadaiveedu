Pazhamudircholai பழமுதிர்சோலை

A forest shrine high in the Alagar hills, above the Vishnu temple of Alagar Kovil. Here Murugan, as a boy in a tree, tested the poet Avvaiyar.

The story of this abode

The great poet Avvaiyar was resting under a naaval (jamun) tree when a cowherd boy in its branches asked her: "Do you want a hot fruit or a cold one?" Puzzled, she asked for a hot fruit, and he shook some down into the sand.

As Avvaiyar blew the sand off them, the boy laughed: "Are they so hot you have to cool them?" The wise poet saw that she had been taught humility, and that the boy was Murugan himself.

The name means "the grove of ripe fruits". The hill is still forested, and the naaval tree is the temple's sacred tree.

What to look for

  • Murugan with both Valli and Deivanai.
  • The naaval tree, the temple's sacred tree.
  • Noopura Gangai, a spring further up the hill, near the Rakkayi Amman shrine.
  • Alagar Kovil at the foot, one of the 108 sacred Vishnu temples.

Rituals explained

Bath at Noopura Gangai

Pilgrims bathe in or collect water from the spring further up the hill, believed to have healing power.

Free.

Mottai (head-shaving)

Hair offering at the temple.

Free at HR&CE temples. Take the official receipt.

Archanai

Chanting of the Lord's names with your name and birth star.

Small fee at the counter.

Combined visit

Pilgrims usually pray at Alagar Kovil (Vishnu) on the way up, a meeting of Shaiva and Vaishnava traditions.

Free.

Facilities and accessibility

Easy for elderly visitors

A motorable road climbs 3.4 km from Alagar Kovil to the temple. From the parking area it is only about 10 steps to the shrine.

Temple busShuttles every 20 minutes or so from the foot of the hill, about 15 minutes up.
By carAllowed up the ghat road, with a small toll.
StepsAbout 10 to the shrine.
MonkeysCommon on the hill. Keep food and bags closed.

Where to stay and eat

Stay

Stay in Madurai, about 20–25 km away. There is no real accommodation on the hill.

Food

Free annadhanam at noon. Small stalls at Alagar Kovil; full meals in Madurai.

Before you go

  • Go in the morning: cooler, and the forest is at its best.
  • Visit Alagar Kovil on the way.
  • Keep snacks hidden from the monkeys.
  • Return downhill before dark.

Our tried-and-tested picks

From our own visit, October 2025

Stay

Madurai Residency, Madurai, near the Periyar bus stand.

Getting there

There are direct buses from the Periyar bus stand. Coming back in the late evening was a little harder, as buses were fewer, but there were plenty of share autos.

Eat

Sree Sabarees, back in Madurai. A very busy, typical Madurai-style vegetarian hotel.

How to reach

By train

Madurai Junction is about 20–25 km away, roughly an hour by bus or taxi.

By air

Madurai airport, about an hour by car.

By road

Town bus 44 from Madurai's Periyar bus stand to Alagar Kovil, then the temple shuttle up the hill.
