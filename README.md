# Aaru Padai · ஆறுபடை வீடு

A pilgrim's guide to the Arupadai Veedu, the six sacred abodes of Lord Murugan in Tamil Nadu.

**Live at [aarupadaiveedu.in](https://aarupadaiveedu.in)**

## What's on the site
- A page for each abode: [Thiruparankundram](https://aarupadaiveedu.in/thiruparankundram), [Thiruchendur](https://aarupadaiveedu.in/thiruchendur), [Palani](https://aarupadaiveedu.in/palani), [Swamimalai](https://aarupadaiveedu.in/swamimalai), [Thiruthani](https://aarupadaiveedu.in/thiruthani) and [Pazhamudircholai](https://aarupadaiveedu.in/pazhamudircholai)
- Darshan timings, ticket costs, rituals, facilities and accessibility, where to stay and how to reach
- A trip planner: choose a starting city, number of days and how you travel, and get a day-by-day route
- Share on WhatsApp buttons and a "Suggest a correction" link on every page

## Corrections
Timings and prices change. If you spot something out of date, use the **Suggest a correction** link on the page concerned.

## About
An independent devotee's guide, not the official temple website. Photos by Ram.

Om Saravana Bhava.
