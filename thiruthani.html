Thiruthani திருத்தணி

Thirumurugatrupadai names the fifth abode Kundruthoradal (குன்றுதோறாடல்), Murugan dancing on every hill, and Thiruthani is traditionally taken to stand for it. Also spelt Tiruttani, Thiruttani and Tiruthani. Murugan here is worshipped as Subramaniya Swamy.

A hill temple about 85 km from Chennai, where Murugan's anger after the battle grew calm, and where he married Valli. The closest abode for pilgrims from the north.

District TiruvallurOpen 5:45 AM – 8:45 PMGeneral darshan FreeClimb 365 steps, or by road

The story of this abode

After the battle with Soorapadman, Murugan came to this hill and his anger cooled. Thanigai means calm, and the place came to be called Thiruthani.

Here he also married Valli, the daughter of a hill tribe chief, whom he had courted in the forests nearby. Thiruthani is the abode of Murugan at peace, with both his consorts.

Tradition also holds that Murugan taught the Tamil language to the sage Agastya on this hill.

What to look for

  • The 365 steps, one for each day of the year.
  • Saravana Poigai, the sacred tank at the foot of the hill.
  • The temple's four enclosures and towers on the hilltop.
  • The steps festival on 31 December, when devotees sing on every step to welcome the new year.

Rituals explained

Mottai (head-shaving)

The tonsure hall is on the way up the hill and works from about 6 AM to 4 PM.

Free since 2021. Take the official receipt and decline other charges.

Kavadi

Paal kavadi and flower kavadi are carried up the steps, especially for Aadi Krithigai.

No ticket.

Wedding prayers

Couples pray here for a happy marriage, and Kalyana utsavam can be booked.

₹2,000 for Kalyana utsavam.

Archanai

Chanting of the Lord's names with your name and birth star. A board at the temple offers archanai in Tamil (தமிழில் அர்ச்சனை).

Small fee at the counter.

Facilities and accessibility

Moderate: steps or road

You can climb the 365 steps or go up the ghat road by car or the Devasthanam bus. Elderly and disabled devotees should take the road; there is still some walking at the top.

Steps365, one for each day of the year.
Ghat roadMotorable to the top. Fee: car ₹50, bus ₹100.
Devasthanam busRuns from the foothill to the hilltop for elderly and disabled devotees.
ParkingLarge lots near the foothill tank and at the hilltop bus stop.

Where to stay and eat

Stay

The Devasthanam has cottages and rooms close to the temple. Many pilgrims come as a day trip from Chennai.

Food

The temple runs annadhanam. Vegetarian hotels are near the foothill and in Thiruthani town.

Before you go

  • An easy day trip from Chennai: leave early, back by evening.
  • Book abhishekam at the counter a day or two ahead.
  • Keep small change for the ghat road toll and counters.
  • Avoid Aadi Krithigai weekend unless you want the festival crowd.

Our tried-and-tested picks

From our own visit, October 2025

Day trip

We did Thiruthani as a day trip from Chennai, with no overnight stay.

Getting there

Train from Chennai to Tiruttani station. Buses to the temple leave right in front of the station and go straight up to the hilltop, so we didn't use the steps.

How to reach

Directions in Google Maps

By train

Tiruttani station is on the Chennai–Arakkonam–Tirupati line, with suburban trains from Chennai Central.

By air

Chennai airport is about 2 hours by road.

By road

About 85 km from Chennai on the Chennai–Tirupati highway. Frequent buses from Chennai, Kanchipuram and Tirupati.
