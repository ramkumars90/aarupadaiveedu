Palani பழனி

Also known as Thiru Aavinankudi (திருவாவினன்குடி), its name in Thirumurugatrupadai; today the name belongs to the shrine at the foot of the hill. Also spelt Pazhani. Murugan here is worshipped as Dhandayuthapani.

The most visited of the six. Murugan stands on a hilltop as Dhandayuthapani, a young renunciant holding only a staff, after giving up the fruit and the family.

The story of this abode

The sage Narada brought a single sacred fruit to Shiva and Parvati. They said whichever son circled the world first would have it. Murugan set off on his peacock. Ganesha simply walked around his parents, saying they were his world, and won the fruit.

Hurt, Murugan gave up everything and came to this hill as a renunciant. Parvati followed and consoled him: "Pazham nee", you yourself are the fruit. That phrase is said to give Palani its name.

The idol is believed to have been made by the Siddhar Bhogar from nine minerals (navapashanam). Bhogar's own shrine is inside the temple. The sweet panchamirtham prasadam is Palani's signature.

What to look for

  • The view from the top over Palani town, the paddy fields and the Kodai hills.
  • Bhogar's shrine in the outer corridor.
  • Panchamirtham, sold at the hill temple's official counters.
  • Thiru Avinankudi temple at the foot of the hill, which tradition counts as the original abode.

Six forms in one day

Daily alangarams அலங்காரம்

At each of the six daily poojas, the priests dress Murugan in a different form. Time your darshan to see the one you want.

  1. 6:40 – 7:15 AMSadhu Sanniyasiசாது சந்நியாசி அலங்காரம்Vila poojaThe renunciant with his staff, as he first came to this hill.
  2. 8:00 – 8:30 AMVedarவேடர் அலங்காரம்Sirukala sandhi poojaThe hunter, the form he took to win Valli.
  3. 9:00 – 9:30 AMBalasubramaniyarபாலசுப்பிரமணியர் அலங்காரம்Kala sandhi poojaThe young boy Subramaniya.
  4. 12:00 – 12:45 PMVaidheekalவைதீகாள் அலங்காரம்Uchikkala poojaA Vedic priest, robed for the noon pooja.
  5. 5:30 – 6:15 PMRajaராஜ அலங்காரம்Sayarakshai poojaThe king, in crown and jewels. The golden chariot follows at 7 PM.
  6. 8:30 – 9:00 PMPushpaபுஷ்ப அலங்காரம்Irakala poojaCovered in flowers before he rests for the night.
Good to know. The official site lists the night pooja at 8:30 PM, after the usual 8:00 PM closing for darshan, so most visitors last see the Raja alangaram. You can attend any pooja with a ₹150 ticket (see costs). Festival days run on a different schedule.

Rituals explained

Mottai (head-shaving)

Palani is one of the busiest places in Tamil Nadu for hair offering. Shave, bathe, then go up for darshan.

Free officially. Visitors report barbers asking for money. Insist on the official receipt.

Kavadi and padayatra

For Thaipoosam, lakhs of devotees walk to Palani from across Tamil Nadu and Kerala, many carrying kavadi.

No ticket. Small fees for some kavadi types.

Panchamirtham

A thick sweet of banana, jaggery, ghee, honey and cardamom, offered and taken home as prasadam.

Buy only at Devasthanam counters.

Archanai

Chanting of the Lord's names with your name and birth star.

Small fee at the counter.

Facilities and accessibility

Moderate: choose your way up

The temple is on a hilltop, about 450 m up. There are four ways up, so almost anyone can make it. Long queues on the hill are the harder part on busy days.

StepsAbout 690 steps. 30–45 minutes for a fit adult. Many go barefoot.
Elephant pathA longer, gentler slope beside the steps.
Winch trainA rail car pulled up the hill. About ₹10, or ₹50 for the fast queue. Runs roughly 5 AM – 9 PM.
Rope carAbout 3 minutes to the top. ₹15, or ₹50 for the fast queue. Runs 7 AM – 9 PM, closed 1:30–2:30 PM for maintenance.
Free mealsThe temple runs annadhanam and gives free milk to children.
Elderly tipRide up by rope car or winch, and walk down the steps if you are able.

Where to stay and eat

Stay

The Devasthanam runs several lodges near the foothill, including Dhandapani Nilayam, Karthikeyan Viduthi, Velavan Viduthi and cottages, at reasonable rates. Book on the temple's HR&CE site. Many private lodges are in Palani town.

Food

The temple serves free annadhanam meals. Vegetarian hotels are plentiful around the foothill and in town.

Before you go

  • On festival days, start before dawn: the temple opens at 4 AM.
  • Take the rope car or winch up if you have elderly family with you.
  • Buy panchamirtham only at the official counters.
  • Carry water for the climb; it gets hot by mid-morning.

How to reach

Directions in Google Maps

By train

Palani station is on the Dindigul–Palakkad line, a short auto ride from the hill.

By air

Coimbatore and Madurai airports are each roughly 3 hours away by road.

By road

Buses run often from Dindigul, Madurai, Coimbatore and Kodaikanal. About 120 km from Madurai.

Nearby

  • Idumban shrine, on the neighbouring hill.
  • Kodaikanal, about 65 km, up in the hills.
