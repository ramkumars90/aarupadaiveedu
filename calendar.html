Auspicious days for Lord Murugan முருகனுக்கு உகந்த நாட்கள்

Krithigai, Sashti, Visakam and the big festivals, from Thaipoosam to Kanda Sashti. Add them to your phone's calendar and they stay up to date on their own.

How to subscribe on your phone
  • iPhone: tap Apple Calendar, then Subscribe.
  • Android: tap Google Calendar. It opens Google Calendar in your browser, because the Android app can't add calendars from a link. Sign in and tap Add. The calendar then appears in the app on your phone.
  • Other apps: tap Copy link and paste it where your app says "Subscribe to calendar" or "Add calendar from URL".
  • Subscribed calendars refresh about once a day, so a change here can take a day to reach your phone.

All dates

Dates follow the Tamil panchangam for Chennai. Some calendars, and some temples, keep a day one day earlier or later, so check with the temple before travelling for a festival event.
