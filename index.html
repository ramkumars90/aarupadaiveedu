Arupadai Veedu pilgrimage guide

Six hills, one sea shore, one Lord

The Arupadai Veedu are the six sacred abodes of Lord Murugan in Tamil Nadu. Pilgrims have walked between them for centuries. This guide helps you understand each abode and plan the journey.

Also spelt Aarupadai Veedu, Arupadaiveedu and Aru Padai Veedu. Often called the six temples of Murugan.

The idea of six abodes comes from Thirumurugatrupadai, the Sangam-era poem by Nakkeerar. It guides a seeker toward Murugan, place by place.
